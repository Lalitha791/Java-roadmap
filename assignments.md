# Day 2 Assignments

Try these yourself before looking at the example programs.

1. **Widening:** Store an `int` value of your choice in a `double` variable. Print both.
2. **Narrowing:** Convert `double 123.456` to `int`. Explain why the result is not rounded.
3. **Double to int:** Read a decimal number using `Scanner`, cast it to `int`, and print both values.
4. **Int to double:** Read an integer and convert it to `double`.
5. **Final:** Declare `final int DAYS_IN_WEEK = 7`. Try changing it and observe the compiler error.
6. **Type promotion:** Add two `byte` variables. Store the result in an `int`. Then try storing it in a `byte` and explain the error.
7. **Salary:** Read basic salary. Calculate HRA at 20%, DA at 10%, and gross salary. Print the gross salary as both `double` and `int`.

## Extra challenge
A shop item costs ₹799.99. Apply a 5% discount and calculate the final price. Print the exact decimal price and its integer-cast value.
